Backup

Entries, petty cash and bill photos are saved on this device in this browser only. They stay after you close the page, but clearing browser data or switching phone or browser removes them. Download a backup regularly; it includes the photos. With cloud sync on (status at the top), entries and petty cash also sync to your private space in Claude and appear on your other devices. Bill photos stay on the device where you added them.

App lock

Ask for a PIN every time this page is opened. This is a screen lock to keep casual viewers out, not encryption. If you forget the PIN you will need to clear this site's data in your browser, so keep a backup file.
